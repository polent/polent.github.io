## Introduction

Today, we dive into a recipe that captures the essence of modern Asian vegan cuisine, blending traditional spices with innovative plant-based ingredients. This Spiced Vegan 'Chicken' Skewers recipe is not just a dish, but a celebration of flavors, textures, and the warmth of a well-crafted marinade.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup textured vegetable protein (TVP), hydrated and drained
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into squares
- 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into squares
- 1 large zucchini, sliced into rounds
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Wooden skewers, soaked in water

## Instructions

1. Preheat your grill or grilling pan to medium-high heat.
2. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, maple syrup, cumin, paprika, coriander, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, lime juice, salt, and pepper to create the marinade.
3. Toss the hydrated TVP, bell peppers, and zucchini in the marinade until well coated.
4. Thread the TVP, bell peppers, and zucchini onto the soaked skewers, alternating between the TVP and vegetables.
5. Grill the skewers for about 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the vegetables are tender and the TVP is nicely charred.
6. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro or a sprinkle of sesame seeds if desired.

Enjoy these delightful skewers as a main dish or a tantalizing appetizer. They're perfect for impressing guests at your next dinner party or enjoying a special meal at home. Experience the joy of modern vegan cooking with every bite.
