## Introduction

Today's food truck special combines the heart-warming spices and textures of African cuisine with a vegan twist, tailored to capture the essence of a truly unique and flavorful experience. Dive into the Vegan African Spice Bowl, a dish that promises a burst of flavors with every spoonful.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked millet
- 1/2 cup red lentils
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 sweet potato, cubed
- 1/2 cup chopped red bell pepper
- 1 cup chopped kale
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Rinse red lentils and cook in vegetable broth until tender, about 20 minutes.
2. While lentils cook, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add cubed sweet potatoes and sauté until slightly tender.
3. Add chopped red bell peppers and spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, smoked paprika) to the pan, and continue to sauté for another 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the cooked millet and cooked lentils into the pan, mixing well to combine all the flavors.
5. Add chopped kale and cook until it wilts, about 3-4 minutes.
6. Season with salt to taste and garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

Enjoy this Vegan African Spice Bowl as a nutritious and flavorful addition to your day. It's perfect for anyone looking to explore new culinary landscapes or simply enjoy a comforting, plant-based meal that satisfies the soul.
