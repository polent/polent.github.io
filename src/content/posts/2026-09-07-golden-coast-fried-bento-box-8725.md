## Introduction

Hey there! Today, I am bringing the golden sunshine of the Pacific Coast right into a bento box. This isn’t your typical takeout; it’s a celebration of crispy textures and vibrant, market-fresh flavors. We are talking masa-crusted avocado, tempura nopales, and a smoky adobo dipping sauce that will make you feel like you are sitting at a sun-drenched picnic table in Ojai. It is unfussy, coastal, and perfectly crunchy.

## Ingredients

- 1 large ripe avocado, sliced into thick wedges
- 1/2 cup heirloom blue corn masa harina
- 1/2 cup chilled sparkling water
- 2 cactus paddles (nopales), thorns removed and sliced into strips
- 1 cup seasonal vegetables like wax beans or shishito peppers
- High-heat neutral oil for frying (like grape seed or sunflower oil)
- 1 cup cooked pinto beans, lightly smashed with a pinch of cumin
- 1 tablespoon chipotle in adobo, finely minced
- 1/2 cup vegan cashew crema or almond yogurt
- 1 tablespoon verjus or white wine vinegar
- Flaky sea salt and the zest of one Ojai lemon
- Fresh cilantro, shaved radishes, and pickled red onions for garnish

## Instructions

- In a small bowl, whisk together the masa harina and chilled sparkling water with a pinch of salt until the batter is smooth and thin enough to coat a spoon.
- Pat the avocado wedges, nopales strips, and seasonal veggies completely dry with a paper towel to ensure the batter sticks.
- Heat about an inch of oil in a heavy-bottomed skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat until a drop of batter sizzles immediately.
- Dip each piece of produce into the masa batter, letting any excess drip off, then carefully place them in the hot oil.
- Fry in small batches until golden and crispy, about 2 to 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a wire rack to maintain the crunch.
- Whisk the minced chipotle and verjus into the vegan crema until smooth.
- To assemble, place a generous scoop of the pinto beans in your bento box, then artfully arrange the fried avocado and vegetables on top.
- Finish with a heavy sprinkle of lemon zest and flaky salt, and serve with the adobo crema and fresh garnishes on the side.

This bento is all about that beautiful balance of crunch and creaminess. Grab a glass of chilled Vermentino or a sparkling citrus water and find a sunny spot to enjoy this little piece of the California coast. I cannot wait to see how yours turns out!
