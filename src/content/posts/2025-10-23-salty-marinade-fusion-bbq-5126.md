## Introduction

Embrace the bold and savory flavors of today's salty marinade with a dish that is as nourishing as it is delicious. This recipe combines the robust essence of Asian-inspired marinades with the comforting texture of American BBQ.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded jackfruit
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon chili flakes
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, hoisin sauce, sesame oil, minced garlic, ginger, chili flakes, brown sugar, and lime juice to create the marinade.
2. Add the shredded jackfruit to the marinade and let it soak for at least 30 minutes.
3. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium heat.
4. Remove the jackfruit from the marinade, reserving the liquid. Grill the jackfruit until it begins to char, about 5-7 minutes on each side.
5. Meanwhile, pour the reserved marinade into a small saucepan. Mix cornstarch with a little water and add to the saucepan. Bring to a simmer and cook until the sauce thickens.
6. Serve the grilled jackfruit topped with the thickened marinade.

Serve this delightful dish at your next gathering or enjoy a special meal with loved ones. Its unique blend of flavors is sure to make it a new favorite!
