## Introduction

Habari! Today I am bringing the salt of the Indian Ocean and the warmth of my charcoal jiko to your table with a burger that bridges tradition and modern gourmet flair. This isn't just a meal; it is a journey from the highland bean fields to the spice-scented streets of Old Town Mombasa. We take the humble njahi—our black bean of strength—and elevate it with the fiery soul of berbere and the sweetness of coastal mango. This is how we celebrate the bounty of Kenya in a single, delicate bite.

## Ingredients

* 2 cups cooked njahi (black beans), mashed slightly but keeping some texture
* 1/2 cup roasted groundnuts (peanuts), crushed into a coarse meal
* 1 small red onion, finely diced
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 tablespoons berbere spice blend
* 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
* 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (or cassava flour for a gluten-free option)
* 1 flax egg (1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tbsp water)
* 4 cardamom-infused sweet potato buns (or vegan brioche)
* 1 cup sukuma wiki (collard greens), finely shredded
* 1 ripe mango, diced for kachumbari
* 1 large tomato, diced
* 1 small red onion, diced for kachumbari
* Fresh cilantro, chopped
* 1 lime, juiced
* 2 pilipili chiles, minced (adjust for heat)
* 1/2 cup thick coconut cream mixed with a pinch of salt and lime zest

## Instructions

* In a large bowl, combine the mashed njahi beans, crushed groundnuts, diced onion, garlic, berbere spice, and tamarind paste. Mix well until the spices are evenly distributed.
* Fold in the flax egg and breadcrumbs. Shape the mixture into four thick, firm patties. Let them rest in the fridge for 20 minutes to set.
* While the patties rest, prepare the kachumbari by tossing the mango, tomato, the second red onion, cilantro, lime juice, and minced pilipili chiles in a small bowl. Set aside to let the flavors meld.
* Lightly sauté the shredded sukuma wiki in a pan with a drop of coconut oil and a pinch of salt just until wilted but still bright green.
* Heat a cast-iron skillet or tava over medium-high heat. Sear the njahi patties for 4-5 minutes per side until a deep, dark crust forms, reminiscent of the char from a jiko.
* Toast the cardamom buns until golden.
* To assemble, spread a generous spoonful of the zesty coconut cream on the bottom bun. Layer the sautéed sukuma wiki, followed by the njahi patty.
* Top with a mountain of the mango kachumbari and place the top bun on gently.
* Serve immediately while the patty is hot and the kachumbari is cool and crisp.

I hope every bite takes you to the white sands of Mombasa and the lush green hills of our highlands. This burger is my heart on a plate, a fusion of trade winds and red soil. Serve this with a side of crispy cassava chips and a squeeze of lime. Until our paths cross again at the communal table, Karibu chakula!
