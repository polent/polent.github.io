## Introduction

Dive into the delightful and vibrant world of African-inspired vegan finger food with this special recipe. Perfect for gatherings or a cozy evening, these Vegan African Spiced Potato Balls encapsulate the rich flavors and textures of traditional African spices blended with the heartiness of potatoes.

## Ingredients

- 2 large potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Salt to taste
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Oil for frying

## Instructions

1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash them until smooth.
2. In a pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, sauté until soft.
3. Add cumin seeds, coriander powder, turmeric, and paprika. Cook for 2 minutes.
4. Mix the sautéed spice mixture into the mashed potatoes. Add salt, cilantro, and lemon juice. Combine well.
5. Form the mixture into small balls.
6. Heat oil in a deep fryer or pan. Fry the potato balls until golden and crispy.
7. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

Enjoy these warm, spiced potato balls as a perfect snack or appetizer. They're sure to bring a burst of African flavor to your table and become a new favorite!
