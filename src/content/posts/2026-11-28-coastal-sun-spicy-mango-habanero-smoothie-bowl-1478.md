## Introduction

Waking up along the coast calls for something that matches the energy of the morning swell. This spicy smoothie bowl leans into the sweet warmth of Central Valley mangoes, cool avocado, and a fiery hit of habanero and ginger, tied together with coastal citrus and crunchy toasted pepitas.

## Ingredients

* 2 cups frozen ripe mango chunks
* 1/2 ripe Haas avocado, scooped
* 1 frozen banana
* 1 fresh lime, juiced
* 1/4 orange, juiced
* 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
* 1/4 fresh habanero pepper, seeded and minced (adjust to heat preference)
* 1/2 cup cold coconut water
* Pinch of sea salt
* 2 tablespoons toasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas), for topping
* 1 tablespoon unsweetened toasted coconut flakes, for topping
* 1/2 teaspoon Tajín or chili-lime seasoning, for topping
* Fresh cilantro or mint leaves, for garnish

## Instructions

* Add the frozen mango chunks, avocado, frozen banana, lime juice, orange juice, grated ginger, minced habanero, sea salt, and cold coconut water to a high-speed blender.
* Blend on low to start, using the tamper to push down the ingredients, then increase to high until the texture is thick, creamy, and spoonable.
* Pour the vibrant golden base into a wide shallow bowl.
* Arrange toasted pumpkin seeds and toasted coconut flakes across the top for crunch.
* Dust lightly with Tajín and finish with a scattering of torn fresh herbs.
* Serve immediately while ice-cold.

Bright, fiery, and deeply refreshing, this bowl brings that farmers market picnic vibe straight to your breakfast table. Grab a spoon, take it out into the sun, and enjoy the contrast of cool creaminess and vibrant heat.
