## Introduction

Hey there! Today we are celebrating that perfect golden hour light with a breaded picnic spread that feels like a salty breeze off the Pacific. I have taken meaty golden oyster mushrooms and given them a crunchy, sun-kissed crust using blue corn masa and almond meal. It is the kind of dish you want to eat with your hands, sitting on a blanket overlooking the coast, dipping each crispy bite into a zesty, charred salsa made from the mornings market haul. This is unfussy coastal living on a plate.

## Ingredients

* 1 lb golden oyster or king trumpet mushrooms, sliced into thick strips
* 1 cup blue corn masa harina
* 1/2 cup almond meal
* 1 tsp smoked pimentón
* 1/2 tsp flaky sea salt
* 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
* 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
* Avocado oil for shallow frying
* 2 ears of corn, husked and charred over an open flame
* 2 large heirloom tomatoes, diced
* 1 Fresno chile, finely minced
* 1/2 cup fresh cilantro and mint, torn
* Juice of 2 Ojai limes
* 2 tbsp Sonoma extra virgin olive oil
* Flaky salt and cracked black pepper to taste

## Instructions

* In a small bowl, whisk the almond milk and apple cider vinegar together and let it sit for 5 minutes to create a quick vegan buttermilk.
* In a shallow dish, combine the blue corn masa harina, almond meal, smoked pimentón, and sea salt.
* Dip each mushroom piece into the almond milk mixture, then dredge thoroughly in the masa mixture, pressing firmly so the breading sticks.
* Heat about half an inch of avocado oil in a heavy cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
* Fry the mushrooms in small batches until they are deeply golden and crispy, roughly 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a wire rack to maintain crunch.
* For the salsa, slice the charred kernels off the corn cobs and toss them in a bowl with the diced tomatoes, Fresno chile, lime juice, and olive oil.
* Fold in the fresh cilantro and mint just before serving to keep the herbs bright.
* Arrange the crispy mushrooms on a wooden board, top generously with the charred corn salsa, and finish with a final sprinkle of sea salt.

Pack these up in a recycled paper box, grab a bottle of chilled Pet-Nat or some sparkling verjus, and head to the nearest park or beach. There is absolutely nothing like the crunch of a masa crust paired with the sweetness of peak-summer tomatoes. Enjoy the sunshine and every crunchy bite!
