## Introduction

Inspired by the zesty and refreshing notes of a citrusy high tea, this unique vegan recipe captures the essence of a light and flavorful afternoon treat. Perfect for those who enjoy a blend of sweet and tangy tastes, this dish is sure to brighten your day and offer a delightful culinary experience.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/3 cup cold vegan butter, cubed
- 1/4 cup organic sugar
- Zest of 1 orange
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 3/4 cup coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Optional: 1/4 cup dried cranberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. Add cold vegan butter and use your fingers to rub it into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. Stir in sugar, orange zest, and lemon zest.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together coconut milk, lemon juice, and vanilla extract.
6. Gradually add the wet ingredients to the dry, stirring until just combined. If using, fold in dried cranberries.
7. Turn the dough onto a floured surface and gently knead just until it forms a smooth ball.
8. Flatten the dough into a 1-inch thick round and use a cookie cutter to cut out scones. Place on the prepared tray.
9. Bake in the preheated oven for 15-17 minutes or until golden brown.
10. Allow to cool slightly before serving, optionally with vegan clotted cream and a drizzle of honey.

Enjoy this vibrant and refreshing dish as a perfect complement to your afternoon tea. It's a wonderful way to bring a touch of sweetness and citrus to your table, making any day feel a bit more special.
