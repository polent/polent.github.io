## Introduction

Indulge in the luxurious flavors of the sea with this vegan twist on a classic buttery seafood platter. This dish combines rich textures and warm, sumptuous tastes, designed to make today truly special.

## Ingredients

- 200g king oyster mushrooms, shredded into strands
- 150g hearts of palm, sliced into medallions
- 100g banana blossoms, lightly shredded
- 2 tbsp nori flakes
- 3 tbsp vegan butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, heat the vegan butter and olive oil over medium heat.
2. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
3. Introduce the shredded king oyster mushrooms, hearts of palm, and banana blossoms to the skillet. Stir well to coat with the butter mixture.
4. Sprinkle in the nori flakes, smoked paprika, and turmeric, mixing thoroughly.
5. Cook for about 8-10 minutes, or until the 'seafood' is tender and infused with flavors.
6. Season with salt and pepper, and squeeze the lemon juice over the mixture.
7. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

Enjoy this vegan seafood delight as a centerpiece for your next gathering or a luxurious weeknight treat. The perfect fusion of comfort and elegance, it's sure to impress both vegans and seafood lovers alike.
