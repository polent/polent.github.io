## Introduction

Hujambo! Today we are capturing the soul of a high-altitude Kenyan coffee—those bright, berry-like notes and the citrus acidity that dances on the tongue. In my Mombasa kitchen, I translate these 'fruity coffee' vibes into a breakfast that marries the tartness of hibiscus and tamarind with the deep, fermented earthiness of teff. It is a dish that feels like the first golden rays of sun hitting the Indian Ocean.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup teff flour (fermented overnight with 1/2 cup water)
- 1/2 cup fresh coconut milk
- 2 firm pears, peeled and halved
- 1/2 cup dried hibiscus flowers
- 1 teaspoon green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
- 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
- 3 tablespoons coconut sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 2 tablespoons toasted groundnuts, roughly crushed
- Fresh mint leaves for garnish

## Instructions

- Prepare the crumpet batter by whisking your fermented teff starter with the coconut milk and sea salt until it reaches a thick but pourable consistency.
- In a small clay pot or saucepan, combine 2 cups of water with the hibiscus flowers, crushed cardamom pods, and tamarind paste.
- Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer and add the pear halves, poaching them for about 12-15 minutes until tender but still holding their shape.
- Carefully remove the pears and set aside; turn up the heat to boil the remaining liquid until it reduces into a thick, glossy ruby-red syrup.
- Lightly grease a tava or flat pan over a medium jiko or stove. Pour small circles of the teff batter, allowing them to cook undisturbed until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look dry.
- Flip the crumpets briefly to finish the other side, then place two on a warm plate.
- Top each crumpet with a poached pear half and drizzle the warm hibiscus-tamarind reduction over the top.
- Finish with a sprinkle of toasted groundnuts and a few mint leaves for a burst of color and texture.

As you enjoy these crumpets, let the tang of the tamarind and the floral scent of cardamom remind you of the spice markets and the red soil of the highlands. It is the perfect companion to your morning brew. Stay vibrant and nourished!

*Nia*
