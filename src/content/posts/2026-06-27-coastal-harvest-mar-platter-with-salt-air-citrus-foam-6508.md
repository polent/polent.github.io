## Introduction

Hey there! Today we're bringing the salt-spray and golden light of the Pacific Coast right to your table. I've taken the concept of a high-end foamed seafood platter and reimagined it with a plant-based soul. We're using king oyster mushrooms and hearts of palm to mimic the textures of the sea, paired with a delicate citrus dulse foam that tastes exactly like a morning walk on a Mendocino beach. It's light, bright, and perfectly unfussy.

## Ingredients

- 4 large king oyster mushrooms, sliced into 1-inch thick rounds
- 1 can (14 oz) hearts of palm, sliced into coins
- 2 heirloom tomatoes, sliced into wedges
- 1 ripe Hass avocado, sliced
- 1 fresno chile, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons Sonoma olive oil
- 1 tablespoon white miso paste
- 1 tablespoon dulse flakes (dried seaweed)
- 1/2 cup aquafaba (liquid from canned chickpeas)
- 1 teaspoon soy lecithin (optional, for foam stability)
- Juice of 2 limes
- 1 tablespoon preserved lemon, minced
- Flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper
- Fresh cilantro and micro-greens for garnish

## Instructions

- Score the top of the mushroom rounds in a crosshatch pattern and whisk together miso, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and a splash of water to create a marinade; coat the mushrooms and let sit for 20 minutes.
- In a small bowl, toss the hearts of palm with lime juice, minced preserved lemon, and dulse flakes to infuse them with a briny, oceanic flavor.
- Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat with the remaining olive oil; sear the mushrooms until golden brown and tender, about 4 minutes per side.
- To create the foam, combine the aquafaba, a squeeze of lime, a pinch of dulse, and soy lecithin in a tall jar; use an immersion blender to aerate the liquid until a stiff, airy foam forms on the surface.
- Arrange the seared mushrooms, marinated hearts of palm, heirloom tomatoes, and avocado on a large ceramic platter.
- Scatter the sliced fresno chiles and micro-greens over the top.
- Just before serving, spoon the citrus dulse foam generously over the mushrooms and hearts of palm.
- Finish with a final drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt.

This platter is all about the contrast between the warm, charred mushrooms and the cool, airy foam. It’s my favorite way to celebrate a sunny afternoon with friends. Grab a chilled glass of Vermentino, find a sunny spot, and dive in. Stay coastal!
