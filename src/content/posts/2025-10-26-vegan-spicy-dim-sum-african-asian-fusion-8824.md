## Introduction

Dive into the spicy world of dim sum with a vegan twist that captures the essence of traditional flavors paired with African culinary artistry. This African-Asian fusion dish blends the spicy, aromatic nature of Asian cuisine with robust African ingredients, creating a unique and delightful dining experience.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup finely chopped collard greens
* 1 cup shredded carrots
* 1/2 cup finely diced sweet potatoes
* 1/2 cup cooked and mashed adzuki beans
* 2 tablespoons finely chopped ginger
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon chili flakes
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* Salt to taste
* 12 vegan wonton wrappers
* 2 tablespoons sesame oil for frying
* Dipping sauce (optional): soy sauce, chili oil, and rice vinegar mixed to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine collard greens, carrots, sweet potatoes, adzuki beans, ginger, and garlic.
2. Add soy sauce, chili flakes, paprika, and salt. Mix thoroughly until the filling is well combined.
3. Take a wonton wrapper and place a tablespoon of the filling in the center. Moisten the edges of the wrapper with water and fold to form a triangle, pressing the edges to seal.
4. Heat sesame oil in a pan over medium heat. Once hot, add the prepared dim sums and cook until golden brown on both sides.
5. Serve hot with the optional dipping sauce.

Savor the rich, multi-layered flavors of this vegan spicy dim sum. Perfect for a cozy dinner or a special occasion, this dish is sure to impress with its unique blend of cultures and tastes.
