## Introduction

Today at the market, the tender baby leeks and wild chanterelles looked like pure gold under the morning sun. I brought them straight home to fold into a buttery, masa-flecked galette crust. Topped with quick-cured Meyer lemon slices and brushed with an earthy, spicy agave-ancho glaze right out of the oven, this tart captures everything I love about coastal California: sunlit produce, deep warmth, and a bright kiss of citrus.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unbleached all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup heirloom yellow masa harina
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 cup cold vegan cultured butter, cubed
- 3-4 tbsp ice-cold sparkling water
- 1/2 cup raw cashews, soaked in boiling water for 20 minutes and drained
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tbsp Sonoma extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 1 Meyer lemon, halved (juice half, slice half paper-thin and seed)
- 1/2 lb golden chanterelle mushrooms, brushed clean and torn into bite-sized pieces
- 3 medium baby leeks or spring onions, trimmed and sliced into 1-inch lengths
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp dark agave nectar
- 1 tsp ancho chile powder
- 1 tbsp aged verjus or apple cider vinegar
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme, leaves picked
- Flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper to taste

## Instructions

- Whisk together the flour, masa harina, and salt in a medium bowl. Work the cubed vegan butter into the flour with your fingertips until pea-sized crumbs form. Drizzle in sparkling water one tablespoon at a time until the dough just comes together. Form into a flat disc, wrap, and chill for at least 30 minutes.
- Blend the soaked cashews, nutritional yeast, 1 tablespoon olive oil, the juice of half the Meyer lemon, and 3 tablespoons water until completely smooth and creamy. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
- Heat a splash of olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the leeks and chanterelles with minced garlic and thyme leaves for 4 to 5 minutes until just tender and edges begin to caramelize. Remove from heat.
- Whisk together the agave nectar, ancho chile powder, and verjus in a small bowl to create the spicy sweet glaze.
- Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Roll the chilled dough out onto a lightly floured surface into a rough 11-inch circle, about 1/8-inch thick, then transfer to the baking sheet.
- Spread the cashew cream over the dough, leaving a 2-inch border. Pile the sautéed leeks, chanterelles, and paper-thin Meyer lemon slices over the cream. Fold the dough edges up and over the filling, pleating gently.
- Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until the pastry is deeply golden and crisp.
- Remove from the oven and immediately brush the warm mushrooms, leeks, and crust edges generously with the ancho-agave glaze. Finish with flaky sea salt and fresh thyme before slicing.

Slice this tart warm while the crust is still delightfully flaky and the glaze catches the afternoon light. Pair it with a chilled glass of dry coastal cider or an estate sauvignon blanc, and share it outside with your favorite people.
