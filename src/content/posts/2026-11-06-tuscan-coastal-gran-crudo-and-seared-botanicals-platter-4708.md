## Introduction

Growing up near the Tyrrhenian coast, the scent of wild rosemary, sea spray, and wood smoke always inspired my kitchen. Today, we honor the sea without taking from it. By uniting thick king oyster mushroom stems, tender hearts of palm, wild salicornia, and sea dulse with cold-pressed Tuscan olive oil, we create a delicate vegan seafood platter that captures the saline breeze, tender sweetness, and rustic elegance of a coastal Italian feast.

## Ingredients

* 3 large king oyster mushroom stems, sliced into 2.5 cm rounds and scored in a fine diamond pattern
* 1 can (400g) whole hearts of palm, cut into delicate calamari-style rings
* 100g fresh salicornia (samphire or sea beans), rinsed well
* 1 tablespoon dried wild dulse or wakame flakes, finely crushed
* 4 tablespoons cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, divided
* 2 cloves garlic, gently bruised
* 1 untreated organic Amalfi lemon, zested and juiced
* 60ml dry Italian white wine, such as Vernaccia di San Gimignano
* 1 tablespoon small salted capers from Pantelleria, rinsed and drained
* 1 tablespoon white shoyu or light tamari
* Flaky sea salt and freshly cracked white pepper to taste
* Fresh wild fennel fronds and micro sea greens for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a ceramic bowl, whisk two tablespoons of olive oil, lemon juice, half of the lemon zest, white shoyu, and crushed seaweed flakes to create an oceanic marinade.
2. Gently fold the hearts of palm rings into the marinade, letting them steep for 20 minutes to absorb the mineral brine of the sea.
3. Heat a heavy cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat with one tablespoon of olive oil and the bruised garlic cloves. Once shimmering, place the scored king oyster mushroom rounds flat side down.
4. Sear undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes until deeply caramelized and golden, then flip. Pour in the white wine, allowing it to reduce vigorously while glazing the tender mushroom scallops.
5. In the final minute, toss the salicornia directly into the pan juices just until warmed through, keeping its crisp, saline crunch intact.
6. Arrange the warm glazed mushroom scallops, marinated chilled hearts of palm, and vibrant salicornia onto a shallow serving dish.
7. Scatter the capers, wild fennel fronds, and remaining lemon zest across the platter, finishing with a final drizzle of fresh Tuscan extra virgin olive oil and a pinch of flaky salt.

Serve this platter immediately with warm crusty sourdough rubbed with raw garlic and a glass of chilled Vermentino. It proves that with reverence for the earth and the mineral depth of ocean botanicals, cucina povera can create unmatched seaside luxury.
