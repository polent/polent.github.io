## Introduction

Fresh off the coast and inspired by the sweet, savory pantry of Northern California, these roasted squash blossoms are stuffed with creamy whipped almond-chèvre, sweet charred corn, and smoky hatch chile. Baked until crisp and blistering, then finished with a drizzle of spicy fermented hot agave and fragrant Sonoma olive oil, they capture the vibrant warmth of an early evening picnic by the Pacific.

## Ingredients

* 12 fresh zucchini squash blossoms, gently cleaned with stamens removed
* 1 cup blanched raw almonds, soaked overnight and drained
* 3 tablespoons filtered water
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (preferably Meyer lemon)
* 1 tablespoon Sonoma extra virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
* 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
* 1 small ear of sweet corn, grilled or charred and kernels cut from cob
* 1 mild Hatch or Anaheim chile, charred, peeled, seeded, and finely diced
* 2 scallions, thinly sliced
* 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh cilantro and mint
* 1 tablespoon blue cornmeal for light dusting
* 1 tablespoon hot agave or habanero-infused maple syrup, for finishing
* Flaky sea salt and lime zest, for serving

## Instructions

* Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper brushed with a light sheen of olive oil.
* In a high-speed blender, combine the soaked almonds, water, lemon juice, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and sea salt. Blend on high until velvety smooth and thick, resembling a spreadable chèvre.
* Transfer the almond cheese to a mixing bowl and gently fold in the charred corn kernels, diced Hatch chile, scallions, and chopped herbs.
* Carefully open the petals of each squash blossom and spoon or pipe about 1 to 1.5 tablespoons of the filling into the center.
* Gently twist the petal ends together to enclose the filling and arrange the blossoms in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
* Lightly brush the stuffed blossoms with olive oil and dust the exterior delicately with blue cornmeal for an earthy crunch.
* Bake for 12 to 14 minutes, or until the blossom tips are golden brown and crisp and the filling is heated through.
* Transfer to a warm serving platter, drizzle with hot agave, and finish with flaky sea salt and freshly grated lime zest.

Serve these golden, blistered beauties straight from the oven while the blossom tips are delightfully crisp and the filling is lush and molten. They pair effortlessly with a crisp chilled vermentino and the lingering ocean breeze.
