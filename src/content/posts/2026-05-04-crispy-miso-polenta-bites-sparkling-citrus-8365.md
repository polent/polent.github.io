## Introduction

Ciao, bellissimi! Today I am channeling the effervescent energy of the latest crispy drink trend—that sharp, cold, and tingling sensation we all crave—and turning it into a bite-sized masterpiece. We are taking the humble Italian polenta, making it incredibly crunchy, and topping it with a zesy gremolata that mimics that citrusy, sparkling bite. It is plant-based, deeply umami, and absolutely vibrant.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup organic coarse cornmeal
* 3 cups filtered vegetable broth
* 2 tablespoons white miso paste
* 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast
* 2 tablespoons tamari or high-quality soy sauce
* 1 tablespoon maple syrup
* 1 teaspoon freshly grated ginger
* 1/2 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
* 1 organic lemon, zested and juiced
* 2 red radishes, cut into tiny matchsticks
* 1 tablespoon pickled ginger, minced
* A splash of sparkling water or kombucha for the topping
* Extra virgin olive oil for shallow frying
* Flaky sea salt to finish

## Instructions

* Bring the vegetable broth to a boil in a medium pot then whisk in the miso paste until fully dissolved.
* Slowly pour in the cornmeal while whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Reduce heat to low and cook for about 20 minutes until the polenta is thick and creamy.
* Stir in the nutritional yeast and a touch of black pepper then spread the mixture into a lined rectangular baking dish at about 1 inch thickness. Let it cool and then refrigerate for at least 1 hour until firm.
* Once firm, cut the polenta into uniform cubes or rectangles.
* In a small bowl, whisk together the tamari, maple syrup, and grated ginger to create your umami glaze.
* Heat a generous amount of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the polenta bites until they are golden and exceptionally crispy on all sides.
* While the polenta fries, prepare the gremolata by mixing the parsley, lemon zest, radishes, and pickled ginger. Just before serving, add a tiny splash of lemon juice and sparkling water to the herb mix for a bright, fizzy sensation.
* Toss the hot polenta bites in the miso-tamari glaze and plate them immediately.
* Top each bite with a spoonful of the sparkling gremolata and a pinch of flaky sea salt.

There you have it—a dish that dances on the tongue just like a cold glass of something sparkling. It is the perfect marriage of my Italian roots and the bold, fermented flavors of the East. Serve these while they are hot and loud, and remember: the secret is in the contrast between the warm crunch and the cold, zingy topping. Buon appetito!
