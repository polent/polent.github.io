## Introduction

Inspired by the warmth and variety of a toasty charcuterie board, this recipe brings together the rich flavors and textures of African cuisine in a plant-based dish. Perfect for a cozy gathering or a special dinner, this vegan charcuterie-inspired platter is sure to delight your taste buds.

## Ingredients

- Teff and millet crackers
- Spiced chickpea and peanut dip
- Marinated olives and artichokes
- Smoked carrot "salmon"
- Cashew cheese with herbs
- Roasted red pepper hummus
- Freshly chopped vegetables (cucumbers, carrots, bell peppers)
- Dried fruits (apricots, figs)
- Nuts (almonds, walnuts)

## Instructions

1. Prepare teff and millet crackers by mixing flours, water, and a pinch of salt. Bake until crispy.
2. Blend chickpeas, peanuts, and spices to make the dip.
3. Marinate olives and artichokes in olive oil, garlic, and herbs.
4. Prepare smoked carrot "salmon" by marinating sliced carrots in smoky seasoning and baking.
5. Blend cashews with herbs and nutritional yeast to create cashew cheese.
6. Make roasted red pepper hummus by blending cooked chickpeas, roasted peppers, tahini, and spices.
7. Chop fresh vegetables for dipping.
8. Arrange dried fruits and nuts on the platter.
9. Assemble all components on a large serving board and enjoy.

Enjoy this delightful vegan twist on a charcuterie board, where every ingredient brings a touch of comfort and culinary innovation to your table. Perfect for sharing with loved ones!
