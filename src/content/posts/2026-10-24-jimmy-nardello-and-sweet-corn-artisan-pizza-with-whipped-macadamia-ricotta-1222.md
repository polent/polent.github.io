## Introduction

A sunlit homage to the coastal market stands—blistered sweet Jimmy Nardello peppers, golden sweet corn, and a velvety whipped macadamia ricotta atop an airy, oak-fired artisan sourdough crust.

## Ingredients

* 500g naturally fermented sourdough pizza dough, room temperature
* 1 cup raw macadamia nuts, soaked in warm water for 2 hours and drained
* 3 tablespoons filtered water
* 1 tablespoon Meyer lemon juice
* 1 teaspoon nutritional yeast
* 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt
* 4 Jimmy Nardello sweet peppers, thinly sliced into rings
* 1 ear fresh sweet corn, kernels sheared from the cob
* 1 small yellow summer squash, shaved into thin ribbons
* 2 tablespoons extra virgin Sonoma olive oil, divided
* 1 tablespoon mild white verjus
* 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves and cilantro blossoms
* 2 tablespoons coarse semolina flour for dusting

## Instructions

* 1. Place a pizza stone or baking steel on the top rack of your oven and preheat to 500°F (260°C) for at least 45 minutes.
* 2. Blend the soaked macadamia nuts, filtered water, Meyer lemon juice, nutritional yeast, and sea salt in a high-speed blender until silky and fluffy.
* 3. Toss the sliced Jimmy Nardello peppers, corn kernels, and shaved summer squash in a mixing bowl with one tablespoon of olive oil and the white verjus.
* 4. Dust a pizza peel with coarse semolina. Gently stretch half of the dough into a 10-inch round, preserving the airy pockets along the outer rim.
* 5. Spoon creamy dollops of whipped macadamia ricotta across the surface, then distribute half of the dressed sweet pepper and corn mixture.
* 6. Slide the pie onto the hot stone and bake for 7 to 9 minutes until the crust is puffed with deep golden leopard spots and vegetables are tender.
* 7. Repeat with the second half of the ingredients, finish both pies with fresh basil, blossoms, flaky sea salt, and a drizzle of Sonoma olive oil.

Slice into this warm, gently sweet pie out on the patio with a glass of crisp Mendocino white wine. Pure California sunshine in every bite!
