## Introduction

Today's seasonal special, the Al Dente Asian BBQ Tempeh Bowl, is a delightful blend of hearty, smoky flavors with a refreshing crunch. Perfect for a cozy meal, this dish brings together the warmth of BBQ with the crispness of fresh vegetables, all served over perfectly cooked al dente noodles.

## Ingredients

- 200g tempeh, sliced
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 100g fresh broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 large carrot, julienned
- 150g buckwheat noodles
- 1 tbsp scallions, chopped
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat sesame oil in a pan over medium heat and add the tempeh slices. Cook until golden on each side.
2. Mix hoisin sauce, soy sauce, smoked paprika, and garlic powder in a bowl to create the BBQ glaze.
3. Brush the tempeh with the BBQ glaze and cook for an additional 2 minutes each side or until caramelized.
4. Boil the buckwheat noodles until al dente, then drain and set aside.
5. Steam the broccoli and carrots until just tender, about 3-4 minutes.
6. Arrange the noodles in a bowl. Top with glazed tempeh, steamed vegetables, scallions, sesame seeds, and fresh cilantro.
