## Introduction

A sun-drenched Pacific morning calls for waking up the senses with vibrant, digestive botanicals. Bitter greens and sharp citrus are undergoing a much-needed renaissance in modern food, and paired with California avocados, they create absolute magic. In this vibrant bowl, mineral-rich dandelion greens and tart Ojai ruby red grapefruit balance buttery Hass avocado, unsweetened heirloom cacao, and toasted spiced pepitas for a textured, revitalizing breakfast.

## Ingredients

* 1 ripe California Hass avocado, pitted and peeled
* 1 cup fresh tender dandelion greens or baby chicory, stems trimmed
* 1 large Ojai ruby red grapefruit, peeled and segmented
* 1 cup frozen green mango chunks
* 1 1/2 tablespoons raw ceremonial-grade cacao powder
* 1/2 inch fresh ginger root, peeled
* 3/4 cup unsweetened chilled almond milk or coconut water
* 1 teaspoon light blue agave nectar (optional, for balance)
* 1 pinch flaky sea salt
* 2 tablespoons raw cacao nibs (for topping)
* 2 tablespoons toasted cumin-dusted pepitas (for topping)
* A few fresh mint leaves (for garnish)

## Instructions

* 1. Combine the dandelion greens, ruby grapefruit segments, peeled avocado, frozen green mango, raw cacao powder, fresh ginger, and flaky sea salt in a high-powered blender.
* 2. Pour in half a cup of the chilled almond milk or coconut water to keep the mixture thick and spoonable.
* 3. Blend on high for 45 to 60 seconds, using the tamper to drive the greens into the blades until the mixture is ultra-creamy and soft-serve thick.
* 4. Add the remaining splash of liquid only if needed to keep things turning, and drizzle in the agave nectar if you prefer a gentler bitter profile.
* 5. Spoon the velvety base into a wide, shallow ceramic bowl.
* 6. Arrange the raw cacao nibs, toasted pepitas, extra ruby grapefruit slices, and fresh mint on top, then serve right away.

Savor this immediately while enjoying the morning breeze. The interplay of bracing bitterness, fragrant coastal citrus, and rich velvety avocado leaves you energized and grounded for whatever adventure the day brings.
