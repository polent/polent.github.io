## Introduction

Today, we're diving into the delightful world of floral-themed dim sum with a vegan twist. This recipe combines the delicate flavors of edible flowers with traditional dim sum ingredients, creating a dish that's not only visually stunning but also bursting with fresh, aromatic flavors.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup wheat starch
- 1/2 cup tapioca starch
- 1 1/4 cups boiling water
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup finely chopped cabbage
- 1/4 cup shredded carrot
- 1/4 cup minced bell peppers
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped chives
- 1/4 cup edible flowers (such as calendula or nasturtiums), chopped
- Soy sauce, for dipping
- Chili oil, optional, for dipping

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix wheat starch, tapioca starch, and salt.
2. Pour boiling water into the starch mixture and stir quickly until the dough forms.
3. Add vegetable oil to the dough and knead until smooth.
4. Divide the dough into small balls, then roll each ball out to form a thin circle.
5. Mix cabbage, carrot, bell peppers, chives, and edible flowers in a bowl for the filling.
6. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each dough circle.
7. Fold the dough over the filling and pleat the edges to seal.
8. Steam the dim sum for 12-15 minutes until translucent.
9. Serve hot with soy sauce and optional chili oil for dipping.

Enjoy this beautiful, floral-inspired dim sum as a special treat that brings a touch of elegance and innovation to your table. Perfect for gatherings or a quiet, refined brunch!
