## Introduction

Embrace the vibrant zest of a citrusy feast with this delightful Citrus-infused Vegan Risotto. Perfect for a refreshing yet comforting meal, this dish blends aromatic citrus flavors with creamy, rich textures, making it a standout choice for any buffet or special day.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Arborio rice
- 3 cups vegetable broth, warmed
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Zest of 1 orange
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup fresh orange juice
- 1/2 cup raw cashews, soaked for 4 hours and drained
- 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh herbs (such as parsley or basil) for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, sauté until translucent.
2. Add the Arborio rice to the pan, stirring to coat the rice in the oil and toast slightly.
3. Begin adding the warm vegetable broth, one ladle at a time, allowing the rice to absorb the liquid before adding more, stirring frequently.
4. While the rice cooks, blend the soaked cashews with lemon juice, orange juice, nutritional yeast, and a pinch of salt until smooth to create a creamy citrus sauce.
5. Once the rice is tender and creamy, stir in the citrus zest and the creamy citrus sauce, adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
6. Serve the risotto garnished with fresh herbs and extra zest if desired.

Serve this bright and creamy Citrus-infused Vegan Risotto and watch it become an instant favorite at your table. Its harmonious blend of fresh flavors and comforting warmth is sure to capture the essence of a truly special day.
