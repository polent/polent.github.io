## Introduction

Benvenuti in cucina! Today, I am bringing the warmth of our Tuscan sun to a bowl of restorative curry. In the spirit of cucina povera, we use what the earth provides: humble cannellini beans and the last of the winter's cavolo nero. By marrying these with golden turmeric and creamy coconut, we create a dish that feels like a hug from the inside. It is simple, soul-warming, and draped in our finest new-press olive oil.

## Ingredients

* 2 cups cooked cannellini beans (or two 15oz cans, rinsed)
* 1 large bunch of cavolo nero (Lacinato kale), stems removed and leaves torn
* 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
* 3 cloves of garlic, minced
* 1 inch piece of fresh ginger, grated
* 1 tablespoon fresh turmeric, grated (or 1 teaspoon ground)
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 can (14oz) full-fat coconut milk
* 1 cup vegetable broth
* 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus more for finishing
* Juice and zest of half a lemon
* Salt and cracked black pepper to taste
* A handful of toasted hazelnuts, crushed for garnish

## Instructions

* Warm the extra virgin olive oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
* Add the onion and sauté until translucent and soft, about 8 minutes.
* Stir in the garlic, ginger, and turmeric, cooking for another 2 minutes until the aroma fills your kitchen.
* Add the cumin and a pinch of salt, stirring to toast the spices briefly.
* Pour in the cannellini beans, vegetable broth, and coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer.
* Let the flavors meld for 15 minutes over low heat, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly.
* Stir in the cavolo nero leaves and cook for 5 minutes until they are tender and bright green.
* Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice and zest.
* Ladle into bowls and finish with a generous drizzle of olive oil, crushed hazelnuts, and a crack of black pepper.

Serve this vibrant bowl with a thick slice of toasted sourdough or a scoop of nutty farro. The secret is the final drizzle of extra virgin olive oil—it brings the whole world back to the hills of Tuscany. Enjoy every spoonful. Buon appetito!
