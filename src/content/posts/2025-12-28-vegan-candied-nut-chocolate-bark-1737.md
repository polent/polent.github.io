## Introduction

Embrace the sweet and savory charm of candied snacks with this delightful plant-based treat. Blending the crunch of candied nuts with the richness of dark chocolate and a hint of sea salt, this vegan candied nut chocolate bark is not only a joy to eat but also simple to make. Perfect for a cozy evening or as a thoughtful homemade gift, it's sure to capture your heart and taste buds.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mixed nuts (such as almonds, pecans, and walnuts)
- 1/2 cup organic brown sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 200 grams dark chocolate (vegan)
- Optional toppings: dried cranberries, pumpkin seeds, or a sprinkle of coarse sea salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the brown sugar and water. Heat over medium until the sugar dissolves, stirring occasionally.
3. Add the nuts and salt to the saucepan, stirring until the nuts are evenly coated.
4. Spread the candied nuts onto the prepared baking sheet in a single layer.
5. Bake for 10-15 minutes, until the nuts are golden and caramelized. Allow to cool.
6. Meanwhile, melt the dark chocolate in a double boiler or microwave, stirring until smooth.
7. Pour the melted chocolate onto a parchment-lined tray, spreading it into an even layer.
8. Sprinkle the candied nuts over the melted chocolate, adding any additional toppings if desired.
9. Place the tray in the refrigerator to set for about 1 hour.
10. Once set, break the bark into pieces and serve.

Enjoy this luscious vegan candied nut chocolate bark as a special treat to sweeten your day or share it with loved ones. It's the perfect blend of textures and flavors, making any moment extra special.
