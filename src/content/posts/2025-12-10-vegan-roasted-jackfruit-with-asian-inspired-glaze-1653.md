## Introduction

Welcome to a comforting and heartwarming vegan roast dinner that captures the essence of traditional flavors with a delightful plant-based twist. This Vegan Roasted Jackfruit with Asian-Inspired Glaze is not only a feast for the eyes but also a treat for the palate, promising to make your dinner time truly special.

## Ingredients

- 1 large jackfruit, peeled and cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp Chinese five spice
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
- Sesame seeds (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. In a large bowl, toss the jackfruit chunks with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread the jackfruit on a baking sheet and roast for 20 minutes, or until it starts to get tender.
4. While the jackfruit roasts, heat sesame oil in a small saucepan over medium heat.
5. Add minced garlic and grated ginger, sautéing until fragrant.
6. Stir in soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar, Chinese five spice, and red pepper flakes, bringing the mixture to a simmer.
7. Let the sauce simmer for about 5 minutes, then remove from heat.
8. Once the jackfruit is tender, remove it from the oven and brush it generously with the glaze.
9. Return the glazed jackfruit to the oven and roast for an additional 10-15 minutes, or until it's caramelized and golden.
10. Garnish with chopped cilantro and sesame seeds before serving.

Serve this delectable Vegan Roasted Jackfruit with your favorite sides for a complete and satisfying meal. It’s perfect for a cozy family dinner or a festive gathering, ensuring everyone leaves the table with a smile.
