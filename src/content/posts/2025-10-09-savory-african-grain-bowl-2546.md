## Introduction

Today's recipe is a vibrant and hearty dish that embodies the spirit of a salty buffet with a healthful, vegan twist. This dish, named 'Savory African Grain Bowl', combines the nuttiness of ancient grains with the robust flavors of spiced vegetables and legumes. It's designed to please both the palate and the eye, making it a perfect addition to your culinary repertoire.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup millet, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup cooked chickpeas
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp coriander
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 bunch kale, destemmed and chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro, for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan, bring the millet, water, and salt to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes until water is absorbed.
2. While the millet cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, sautéing until translucent.
3. Add the red bell pepper, chickpeas, smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, and chili powder. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the sweet potato and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until sweet potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
5. Stir in the chopped kale and cook until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Serve the vegetable and chickpea mixture over the cooked millet. Garnish with fresh cilantro.

Enjoy this delightful Savory African Grain Bowl as a standalone meal or as part of a larger buffet. It's guaranteed to bring a touch of Africa's warmth and richness to your table, wrapped in a healthy, plant-based package. Perfect for any day that calls for a special meal!
