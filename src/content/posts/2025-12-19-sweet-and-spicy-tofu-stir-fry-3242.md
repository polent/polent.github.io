## Introduction

Tonight's dinner is a delightful fusion of zest and warmth, blending the vibrant flavors of Asian cuisine with the wholesomeness of vegan ingredients. This Sweet and Spicy Tofu Stir-fry is not only delicious but also visually appealing, making it a perfect centerpiece for your dinner table.

## Ingredients

- 400g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 medium carrots, julienne
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 inches fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon chili paste
- Juice of one lime
- Fresh coriander for garnish
- Sesame seeds for garnish

## Instructions

1. Toss cubed tofu in cornstarch until evenly coated.
2. Heat sesame oil in a large pan over medium heat and fry tofu until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add more oil if needed, and sauté onions, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
4. Add the bell peppers and carrots to the pan, cooking until just tender.
5. Return the tofu to the pan. Mix in soy sauce, maple syrup, chili paste, and lime juice.
6. Stir everything together and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes until the sauce is thickened.
7. Garnish with fresh coriander and sesame seeds before serving.

Enjoy this Sweet and Spicy Tofu Stir-fry as a standalone dish or pair it with steamed jasmine rice for a more fulfilling meal. It's a great way to spice up your dinner routine while keeping things light and healthy.
