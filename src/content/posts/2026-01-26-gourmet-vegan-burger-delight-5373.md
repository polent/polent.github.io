## Introduction

Dive into the flavors of a gourmet vegan burger with this delicious recipe, designed to satisfy your cravings with a plant-based twist. Featuring a juicy, flavor-packed patty and a delightful array of toppings, this burger is not just a meal but a celebration of texture and taste.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked and mashed black beans
- 1/2 cup finely grated beetroot
- 1/4 cup finely chopped walnuts
- 1/4 cup bread crumbs
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 vegan burger buns
- Lettuce, sliced tomatoes, and red onions for garnish
- Vegan mayonnaise and mustard
- Avocado slices or vegan cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine mashed black beans, grated beetroot, chopped walnuts, bread crumbs, nutritional yeast, smoked paprika, and garlic powder.
2. Season with salt and pepper, and mix until well combined.
3. Form the mixture into four even patties.
4. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and cook the patties for 4-5 minutes on each side or until they are firm and have a crispy outer layer.
5. Toast the burger buns lightly.
6. Spread vegan mayonnaise and mustard on the buns, then layer with lettuce, tomato slices, and red onion.
7. Place a cooked patty on each bun and top with avocado slices or vegan cheese if using.
8. Serve immediately.

Enjoy your gourmet vegan burger with a side of sweet potato fries or a fresh salad for a complete meal. Perfect for a cozy evening or a special weekend treat!
