## Introduction

Jambo! I'm Nia, and today I’m inviting you to experience the 'airy' side of African cuisine. Inspired by the light, frothy textures trending in modern global gastronomy, I’ve crafted a soup that bridges the gap between the coastal flavors of Kenya and the delicate soup traditions of East Asia. This Sahari Airy Coconut and Moringa Broth is an ethereal, foam-topped delight that uses the 'miracle tree' Moringa for a nutrient-packed, vibrant green finish that’s as light as a cloud.

## Ingredients

* 2 cups fresh Moringa leaves (or 2 tablespoons organic Moringa powder)
* 1 can (400ml) light organic coconut milk
* 3 cups vegetable dashi or low-sodium vegetable stock
* 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised and finely minced
* 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 small shallot, finely diced
* 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
* 1 tablespoon lime juice
* 1/2 teaspoon bird's eye chili, finely chopped (optional for heat)
* Sea salt to taste
* Microgreens and toasted sesame seeds for garnish

## Instructions

* Heat the toasted sesame oil in a deep pot over medium heat and sauté the shallot, ginger, garlic, and lemongrass until fragrant but not browned.
* Pour in the vegetable stock and the light coconut milk, bringing the mixture to a gentle simmer for about 10 minutes to infuse the aromatics.
* Stir in the Moringa leaves (or powder) and the bird's eye chili, cooking for just 2-3 minutes to preserve the bright green color and delicate nutrients.
* Remove the pot from heat and stir in the fresh lime juice and sea salt.
* Using high-speed immersion blender, blend the soup on the highest setting, tilting the blender slightly to incorporate air until a thick, frothy foam forms on the surface.
* Carefully ladle the warm liquid into shallow bowls, ensuring each serving gets a generous topping of the light Moringa-coconut foam.
* Garnish with microgreens and a sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds to complete the Afro-Asian aesthetic.

This soup is a testament to how traditional African ingredients like Moringa and coconut can be reimagined into something sophisticated and light. It’s perfect as a starter for a fusion dinner or a restorative midday pick-me-up. I hope this bowl brings a bit of Kenyan sunshine and modern flair to your table. Asante sana!

*Nia*
