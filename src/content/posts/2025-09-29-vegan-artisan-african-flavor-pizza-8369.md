## Introduction

Embark on a culinary journey with this unique artisan vegan pizza, where the richness of African flavors meets the classic, beloved Italian dish. This recipe blends traditional African ingredients into a modern, flaky pizza base, creating a delightful fusion that's sure to warm your heart and tantalize your taste buds.

## Ingredients

- Flaky pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
- 1 cup of cooked and mashed sweet potato
- 1/2 cup of red onions, thinly sliced
- 1 cup of spinach, washed and chopped
- 1/2 cup of roasted red peppers, sliced
- 1/4 cup of sun-dried tomatoes
- 1/2 cup of vegan mozzarella cheese, shredded
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon of mixed dried herbs (oregano, basil, thyme)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Roll out the pizza dough into a thin circle on a floured surface.
3. Spread the mashed sweet potato evenly as the base layer on the dough.
4. Top with spinach, red onions, roasted red peppers, and sun-dried tomatoes.
5. Sprinkle vegan mozzarella cheese over the vegetables.
6. Drizzle olive oil all over and sprinkle with minced garlic and mixed herbs.
7. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Bake in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes, or until the crust is golden and crispy.
9. Remove from oven and let it cool slightly before serving.

Serve this scrumptious vegan artisan pizza fresh out of the oven, topped with a sprinkle of fresh herbs for an added burst of flavor. Perfect for a cozy dinner, it's a comforting yet exciting dish that brings a piece of Africa to your table.
