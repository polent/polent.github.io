## Introduction

Dive into the delightful fusion of Italian elegance and Asian flair with this Vegan Bento-inspired Pasta Box. This recipe combines the comforting textures of pasta with vibrant, fresh vegetables and rich, umami flavors, making it a perfect lunch or dinner option.

## Ingredients

- 200g handmade vegan pasta (linguine or spaghetti)
- 100g tempeh, thinly sliced
- 1 medium carrot, julienned
- 1 small bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 50g baby spinach
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon miso paste
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente, then drain and set aside.
2. In a pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the tempeh slices and fry until crispy. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add garlic and ginger, sautéing until fragrant.
4. Add the carrots and bell peppers, cooking until just tender.
5. Stir in the baby spinach and cook until wilted.
6. In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce and miso paste until smooth. Pour this mixture into the pan with vegetables and allow to simmer for a few minutes.
7. Toss the cooked pasta into the pan, mixing well to coat with the sauce and vegetables.
8. Serve the pasta in a bento-style box, topped with crispy tempeh, sesame seeds, and fresh cilantro.

Enjoy your Vegan Bento-Inspired Pasta Box! This dish is not only a feast for the eyes but also a delightful journey for your taste buds, blending the best of Italian pasta with the vibrant essence of a bento box.
