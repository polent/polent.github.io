## Introduction

Today's food truck special is all about celebrating the salty and savory flavors with a vegan twist. This recipe brings the comfort of American BBQ and the exotic zest of Asian cuisine into a delightful dish that's sure to tantalize your taste buds.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb vegan ribs (seitan based)\n- 2 tbsp soy sauce\n- 1 tbsp miso paste\n- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce\n- 1 tsp smoked paprika\n- 1 tsp garlic powder\n- 1/2 tsp chili flakes\n- 2 tbsp maple syrup\n- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar\n- 1 tsp sesame oil\n- Sesame seeds for garnish\n- Sliced green onions for garnish

## Instructions

1. Preheat your grill to medium-high heat.\n2. In a bowl, mix soy sauce, miso paste, hoisin sauce, smoked paprika, garlic powder, chili flakes, maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, and sesame oil to make the marinade.\n3. Brush the vegan ribs generously with the marinade.\n4. Grill the ribs for about 5-7 minutes on each side until charred and heated through.\n5. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions before serving.

Enjoy this unique fusion dish that not only satisfies your salty cravings but also adds a healthy, plant-based twist to your meal. Perfect for any day when you need a special treat!
