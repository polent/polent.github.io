## Introduction

Today's gourmet sandwich recipe brings a flaky, delicious twist to your lunch! Featuring a fusion of Asian flavors and hearty vegan ingredients, this sandwich is sure to be a standout in your culinary adventures.

## Ingredients

- 2 large vegan flaky pastry sheets 
- 1 cup smoked seitan, thinly sliced 
- 1/2 cup vegan mozzarella cheese, shredded 
- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce 
- 1 tablespoon sriracha 
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped 
- 1/4 cup pickled carrots and daikon radish 
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil 
- 1 teaspoon black sesame seeds 
- Vegan butter for brushing pastry

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). 
2. Roll out the pastry sheets and cut each into 4 squares. 
3. In a small bowl, mix hoisin sauce and sriracha. 
4. Brush each pastry square lightly with sesame oil, then spread a thin layer of the hoisin-sriracha mixture. 
5. Evenly distribute the smoked seitan and vegan mozzarella on each square. 
6. Fold the squares diagonally to form triangles, sealing the edges by pressing with a fork. 
7. Brush the top of each pastry with vegan butter and sprinkle with black sesame seeds. 
8. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until golden and flaky. 
9. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro and pickled vegetables.

Enjoy this delightful sandwich warm, paired with a side of your favorite vegan soup or salad for a complete meal. It's not just a sandwich; it's a celebration of flavors!
