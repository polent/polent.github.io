## Introduction

Out here on the coast, the boundary between savory and sweet is delightfully fluid. Pickling fruit isn't just an old preservation trick—it's an electric way to wake up summer produce. We are steeping sweet strawberries in a bright brine of California verjus, floral hibiscus, and pink peppercorn, then pairing them with an airy Meyer lemon crema and toasted masa crumble for warmth and texture. It is sunlit, punchy, and effortlessly coastal.

## Ingredients

* 1 lb fresh ripe organic strawberries, hulled and halved
* 1/2 cup California white verjus (or mild raw apple cider vinegar)
* 1/4 cup filtered water
* 1/3 cup organic cane sugar
* 1 tbsp dried hibiscus flowers
* 1/2 tsp crushed pink peppercorns
* 1 wide strip Meyer lemon peel
* 1 cup raw cashews, soaked in boiling water for 30 minutes and drained
* 1/2 cup chilled full-fat coconut cream
* 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
* 1 tsp Meyer lemon zest
* 1 tbsp fresh Meyer lemon juice
* 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
* 1/3 cup masa harina
* 2 tbsp almond flour
* 2 tbsp California extra virgin olive oil
* 1 tbsp maple sugar or coconut sugar
* 1/4 tsp flaky sea salt
* Fresh micro-basil or mint leaves, for garnish

## Instructions

* Combine the verjus, water, cane sugar, dried hibiscus flowers, crushed pink peppercorns, and Meyer lemon peel in a small saucepan over medium heat, simmering just until the sugar is dissolved.
* Remove the brine from heat and let steep for 10 minutes to draw out the hibiscus hue and aromatics, then strain through a fine mesh sieve into a heatproof container.
* Place the halved strawberries in a clean heatproof glass dish and pour the warm brine directly over them, letting them cool on the counter before transferring to the refrigerator to chill for at least 1 hour.
* Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
* In a small mixing bowl, stir together the masa harina, almond flour, extra virgin olive oil, maple sugar, and flaky sea salt until coarse, golden crumbs form.
* Spread the masa mixture in an even layer on the baking sheet and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, tossing once halfway through, until crisp and nutty; set aside to cool completely.
* Blend the soaked cashews, coconut cream, maple syrup, lemon zest, lemon juice, and vanilla bean paste in a high-speed blender until velvety and satin-smooth, then chill for 20 minutes to set.
* Spoon a generous swoosh of Meyer lemon crema into shallow ceramic bowls, arrange the drained pickled strawberries across the top, drizzle with a spoonful of the ruby pickling syrup, and finish with the toasted masa crumble and fresh herbs.

Every bite captures the spirit of an early evening market run: bright acidity cutting through silky, rich cream, grounded by the earthy aroma of toasted corn. Keep any extra pickled berries and their jewel-toned syrup in the fridge to spoon over grilled peaches, sourdough toast, or chilled coconut yogurt whenever you want a vibrant pop of California flavor.
