## Introduction

Today's food truck special is a vibrant, plant-based dish that captures the heart of African cuisine with a delightful twist suitable for the Asian palate. This recipe, a Vegan Ethiopian Spiced Mushroom Tibs, is not only a celebration of flavors but also a wholesome, nourishing choice that will light up your taste buds.

## Ingredients

- 500g button mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons berbere spice mix
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
- Juice of one lemon

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onions and cook until they are translucent.
3. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Add the sliced mushrooms to the skillet. Cook until the mushrooms are golden and their moisture has evaporated.
5. Sprinkle the berbere spice mix, smoked paprika, and ground cumin over the mushrooms. Stir well to coat the mushrooms evenly with the spices.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Remove from heat and squeeze the lemon juice over the cooked mushrooms.
8. Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

Enjoy this warm, aromatic dish that brings a piece of Africa to your plate with a healthy, vegan twist. Perfect for a cozy meal or a special occasion, these Ethiopian Spiced Mushroom Tibs are sure to be a new favorite!
