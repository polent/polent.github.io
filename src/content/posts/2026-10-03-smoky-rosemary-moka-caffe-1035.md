## Introduction

Benvenuti in my kitchen. Today, we capture the essence of the Tuscan morning mist and the lingering scent of the wood-fired forno. This Smoky Rosemary Moka Caffè is a tribute to the simplicity of my garden and the deep, charred notes that define our rural life. It is not just a drink; it is an invitation to slow down and breathe in the hills.

## Ingredients

* 1 shot of strong espresso or 1/2 cup deep Moka pot coffee
* 1 sprig of fresh organic rosemary from the garden
* 1 teaspoon dark maple syrup or saba (concentrated grape must)
* 1/2 cup creamy barista-style oat milk
* A tiny pinch of smoked Maldon sea salt

## Instructions

* Prepare your coffee using a traditional Moka pot for that authentic, concentrated Italian flavor.
* Take the rosemary sprig and carefully char the leaves over an open gas flame or with a kitchen torch until they begin to smoke and release their oils.
* Place the smoking rosemary into your favorite ceramic mug and pour the hot coffee directly over it, allowing it to steep for exactly 45 seconds.
* Remove the rosemary sprig and stir in the maple syrup and the tiny pinch of smoked sea salt to enhance the charred depth.
* Froth the oat milk until it reaches a velvety, micro-foam consistency.
* Pour the milk gently over the infused coffee and serve immediately while the smoky aroma is at its peak.

Enjoy this slow-sipped, letting the aroma transport you to a stone terrace in Tuscany. It is simple, honest, and full of soul. Until next time, keep your garden close and your coffee dark. Buon appetito!

*Isabella*
