## Introduction

In shojin ryori, we honor shun—the fleeting perfection of the present season. This zuke-style marinated nigiri transforms king oyster mushroom fillets through binchotan charcoal searing and a quiet bath in aged tamari kombu dashi, drawing forth oceanic depth and delicate chew without taking life.

## Ingredients

* 2 large king oyster mushrooms (eryngii), cut lengthwise into 1/4-inch thick fillets and scored in a fine diamond pattern
* 200 ml cold-brewed Rishiri kombu dashi
* 3 tbsp aged tamari soy sauce
* 2 tbsp sweet mirin
* 1 strip dried kombu (2 inches)
* 2 cups cooked short-grain Japanese sushi rice
* 2 tbsp akazu (aged red sushi vinegar) blended with 1 tsp natural sea salt
* Freshly grated hon-wasabi
* 6 fresh kinome (sansho) leaves, lightly clapped to release oils

## Instructions

* Score the cut surfaces of the mushroom fillets with precise, shallow diagonal knife strokes to absorb the marinade.
* Sear the fillets briefly over binchotan charcoal or a seasoned cast-iron skillet until blistered, fragrant, and slightly tender, about 2 minutes per side.
* In a small pot, simmer the kombu dashi, aged tamari, and mirin for 3 minutes, then take off the heat to cool completely with the dried kombu strip inside.
* Submerge the charred mushroom slices into the cooled dashi marinade, cover, and let steep in the refrigerator for 4 hours to cure.
* Gently season the warm sushi rice with the akazu and sea salt mixture, cutting and folding with a wooden paddle until glossy and held at body temperature.
* Dampen your hands, gently form 20 grams of rice into an oblong nigiri pillow, and place a tiny dab of freshly grated wasabi on top.
* Lay a drained, marinated mushroom fillet over the rice, gently pressing the sides to bind, brush with a drop of reserved marinade, and crown with a fresh kinome leaf.

Enjoy each nigiri within moments of shaping, while the akazu rice holds gentle warmth against the chilled, dashi-soaked jewel above. May this simple offering bring quiet reflection and mindful gratitude to your day.

*Hiroshi*
