## Introduction

Infuse your dinner with a burst of citrus and the vibrant tastes of Africa with this special dish. Perfect for those who love a tangy, flavorful meal, this recipe combines the zest of citrus fruits with hearty African ingredients, creating a delightful vegan experience.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked sorghum
- 1 large orange, zested and juiced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup diced red onion
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped for garnish

## Instructions

- In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
- Add the red onion and garlic, sauté until the onion becomes translucent.
- Stir in the red and yellow bell peppers, cooking until they are tender.
- Add the ground cumin, smoked paprika, ground coriander, and cayenne pepper, cooking for another minute until fragrant.
- Mix in the cooked sorghum, chickpeas, orange juice, lemon juice, lime juice, and their zests. Cook for 10 minutes, allowing the flavors to blend together.
- Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
- Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

Serve this warm, citrus-infused meal to brighten up your dinner table. It's sure to impress with its unique blend of flavors and will become a beloved addition to your culinary repertoire.
