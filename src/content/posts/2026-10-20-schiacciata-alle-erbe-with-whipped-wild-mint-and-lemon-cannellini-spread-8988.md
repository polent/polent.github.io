## Introduction

Ciao amiche e amici! Out here in the Tuscan hills, nothing beats packing a woven basket with warm flatbread pressed with fresh garden sprigs and a velvety spread made from humble cannellini beans. This herbal picnic centerpiece combines wild mint, tender rosemary, and peppery summer herbs with creamy legumes whipped until airy with cold-pressed olive oil and unwaxed lemon zest. It is rustic, vibrant, and built for slow, sunlit afternoons under the olive trees.

## Ingredients

* 350 g stone-ground bread flour
* 260 ml lukewarm water
* 5 g active dry yeast
* 1 tsp unrefined fine sea salt
* 6 tbsp cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, divided
* 1 small bunch fresh garden herbs (rosemary sprigs, fresh sage leaves, lemon thyme, wild fennel fronds)
* 1 tsp flaky sea salt, for topping
* 400 g cooked cannellini beans, rinsed and thoroughly drained
* 1 medium clove garlic, germ removed
* Zest and juice of 1 organic unwaxed lemon
* 15 g fresh wild mint leaves, finely bruised
* 2 tbsp lightly toasted pine nuts, for garnish
* Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

* In a mixing bowl, combine lukewarm water, yeast, and a spoonful of flour, letting it bloom for 10 minutes until frothy.
* Add the remaining flour, fine sea salt, and 2 tablespoons of olive oil, stirring with a wooden spoon until a soft, shaggy dough forms, then let rest covered for 30 minutes.
* Perform three gentle sets of stretch-and-folds inside the bowl every 20 minutes using damp hands, then cover and allow the dough to double in size for about 90 minutes.
* Coat a shallow baking pan with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, tip in the dough, turning once to coat, and let it proof undisturbed for 45 minutes until light and bubbly.
* Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F). Dimple the dough deeply with your fingers, gently press sprigs of rosemary, sage, lemon thyme, and wild fennel into the indentations, drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
* Bake for 22 to 25 minutes until puffed, golden, and crisp around the edges, then let cool slightly on a rack.
* For the spread, place the cannellini beans, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and a generous pinch of fine salt into a high-speed blender or food processor.
* Blend on high until completely silky and whipped, drizzling in 1 to 2 tablespoons of iced water if needed to achieve a cloud-like texture.
* Fold the bruised wild mint and cracked black pepper into the spread by hand to preserve the fresh herbal aroma.
* Spoon the whipped cannellini into a shallow ceramic bowl or transport jar, finish with toasted pine nuts, fresh herb leaves, and a swirl of olive oil, and serve alongside thick slices of the warm herb schiacciata.

Wrap the warm schiacciata in a clean linen towel, pack the whipped bean spread into an airtight jar, and uncork a bottle of chilled Vernaccia. Food shared in the open air, created from garden greens, pantry legumes, and fine olive oil, reminds us that the simplest traditions make the most unforgettable feasts. Buon appetito!
