## Introduction

Hujambo! From my kitchen in Mombasa, where the humid air carries the scent of salt and charcoal, I bring you a morning ritual that wakes up the soul. This is not your typical sweet fruit bowl; it is a Pwani-style awakening. We use the sharp, pungent heat of fresh ginger and our coastal berbere blend to cut through the richness of hand-pressed coconut milk and sun-ripened mango. It is a dish that tastes of the Indian Ocean trade winds and the strength of the red highlands soil.

## Ingredients

* 2 cups frozen mango chunks
* 1 cup thick fresh coconut milk
* 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
* 1 inch fresh ginger, peeled and grated
* 1/2 teaspoon berbere spice blend
* 1 tablespoon baobab powder
* 1/4 cup roasted groundnuts, roughly crushed
* 2 tablespoons toasted coconut flakes
* 1 fresh passion fruit, halved
* A pinch of sea salt

## Instructions

* Begin by placing the frozen mango chunks into a high-speed blender.
* Pour in the thick coconut milk and add the grated ginger, tamarind paste, baobab powder, and the berbere spice.
* Blend on high speed, using a tamper if necessary, until the mixture is thick, creamy, and holds its shape.
* Taste the base; the pungency of the ginger and the warmth of the berbere should balance the sweetness of the mango.
* Scoop the mixture into a chilled earthenware or wooden bowl.
* Top one side with a neat row of crushed roasted groundnuts for a deep, earthy crunch.
* Sprinkle the toasted coconut flakes alongside the nuts.
* Scoop the tart, aromatic seeds of the passion fruit over the center.
* Finish with a tiny pinch of sea salt to elevate the spice and serve immediately before the coastal heat melts your creation.

As you finish the last spoonful of this vibrant bowl, let the warmth of the ginger and the tingle of the berbere linger. It is a reminder that breakfast can be both a comfort and an adventure. May your day be as bold and bright as these coastal flavors. Karibu tena to my table!
