## Introduction

Today we're diving into a dish that embodies the essence of a wet culinary adventure with an Asian-American BBQ twist. Introducing the 'Smokey Maple Miso Tofu Steaks', a perfect blend of hearty, smoky flavors with a touch of sweetness, sure to make your rainy day cozy and delicious.

## Ingredients

- 4 slices of firm tofu (about 1 inch thick each)
- 2 tablespoons miso paste
- 3 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon liquid smoke
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Fresh scallions for garnish
- Sesame seeds for garnish

## Instructions

1. Press the tofu slices between paper towels to remove excess moisture.
2. In a bowl, mix miso paste, maple syrup, soy sauce, liquid smoke, garlic powder, and onion powder to create the marinade.
3. Coat the tofu slices evenly with the marinade and let them sit for at least 30 minutes.
4. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
5. Place the marinated tofu in the skillet and cook each side for about 4-5 minutes or until crispy and golden brown.
6. Garnish with sliced scallions and sesame seeds before serving.

Enjoy these Smokey Maple Miso Tofu Steaks as a stand-alone delight or pair them with your favorite vegan sides. It's a versatile dish that's bound to be a hit, whether it's a cozy meal at home or the centerpiece for your next gathering!
