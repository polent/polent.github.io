## Introduction

Embrace the warmth and vibrant flavors of Africa with this unique dish inspired by today's garnished drink. This recipe is crafted to bring out a symphony of tastes and textures, making it a delightful culinary experience.

## Ingredients

- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 2 teaspoons espresso powder
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Toasted coconut flakes for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, combine coconut milk, vegetable broth, and espresso powder.
2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.
3. Add ginger, garlic, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cardamom.
4. Simmer for 10 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
5. Stir in maple syrup and season with salt to taste.
6. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro and toasted coconut flakes.

Enjoy this delightful dish as a centerpiece for a special meal or as a comforting treat on a chilly day. Its rich flavors and aromatic spices are sure to make today truly special.
