## Introduction

Welcome to a celebration of the soil. Today, we are taking the ancient, resilient grain known as fonio and transforming it into a contemporary masterpiece. This infused casserole is a symphony of smoky harissa, creamy coconut, and the grounding sweetness of roasted African tubers. It is designed to nourish your spirit while honoring the sustainable traditions of my ancestors, bringing a piece of the Kenyan highlands to your modern table.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup fonio, thoroughly rinsed
* 2 cups organic vegetable broth
* 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
* 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and patted dry
* 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
* 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
* 2 tablespoons smoky harissa paste
* 1 tablespoon berbere spice blend
* 1 cup full-fat coconut milk
* 2 cups fresh kale, stems removed and chopped
* 1/4 cup toasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
* 1 tablespoon cold-pressed avocado oil
* Fresh cilantro and lime wedges for garnish

## Instructions

1. Pre-heat your oven to 200C (400F).
2. On a large baking tray, toss the cubed sweet potatoes and red onions with avocado oil and half of the berbere spice. Roast for 20 minutes until the edges are caramelized.
3. While the vegetables roast, bring the vegetable broth to a rolling boil in a small pot. Stir in the fonio, cover with a tight lid, and remove from heat immediately. Let it sit for 5 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork.
4. In a small bowl, whisk together the coconut milk, harissa paste, minced garlic, and the remaining berbere spice until smooth.
5. In a lightly greased casserole dish, create a base layer with the fluffed fonio.
6. Add the roasted sweet potatoes, onions, chickpeas, and chopped kale on top of the fonio layer.
7. Pour the harissa-coconut cream mixture evenly over the vegetables and grains.
8. Place the dish in the oven and bake for 15 to 20 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and the kale is slightly crisp.
9. Remove from the oven and sprinkle with toasted pumpkin seeds and fresh cilantro.
10. Serve warm with a squeeze of fresh lime to brighten the deep, smoky flavors.

As you pull this golden, bubbling dish from the oven, take a moment to breathe in the aroma of spices that have traveled across oceans and generations. This casserole is more than just a meal; it is a bridge between the past and the future of plant-based eating. I hope it inspires you to experiment with heritage grains and find the soul-warming power in every bite. Until next time, keep cooking with heart and heritage.
