## Introduction

In Tuscany, we believe in honoring simplicity, seasonal vitality, and textural contrast. Taking inspiration from the beloved crispy encrusted taco trend, I press artisan tortillas into golden polenta, wild oregano, and fragrant rosemary, searing them in a sizzling skillet until shatteringly crisp. Inside, creamy cannellini beans and braised cavolo nero meet a silky sun-dried tomato and walnut crema for a soul-nourishing bite.

## Ingredients

* 8 small organic corn tortillas
* 1/2 cup fine yellow polenta or cornmeal
* 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast
* 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh rosemary
* 1 teaspoon dried wild oregano
* 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt, plus more to taste
* 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, divided
* 2 cups cooked cannellini beans, lightly crushed
* 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
* 1 bunch cavolo nero (Tuscan kale), stemmed and thinly ribboned
* 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes
* 1/3 cup raw walnuts, soaked in hot water for 15 minutes
* 4 oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
* 3 to 4 tablespoons water

## Instructions

* Blend the soaked walnuts, sun-dried tomatoes, lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and 3 to 4 tablespoons of water in a small blender until completely smooth to create the crema, then set aside.
* Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
* Add the ribboned cavolo nero with a splash of water, cooking for 3 to 4 minutes until tender, then fold in the lightly crushed cannellini beans, season with salt, and keep warm.
* On a shallow plate, mix together the fine polenta, nutritional yeast, minced rosemary, dried oregano, and 1/2 teaspoon sea salt.
* Brush the exterior of each tortilla lightly with olive oil and press firmly into the polenta mixture so it forms an even crust on one side.
* Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat with a light coating of olive oil, then place tortillas crust-side down for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crackling crisp.
* Flip briefly for 30 seconds to warm the inside, then lift and gently fold into taco shapes while warm.
* Spoon the warm cannellini and braised greens into each crunchy shell, drizzle generously with the sun-dried tomato crema, and finish with a drop of fresh olive oil.

Serve these tacos piping hot straight from the skillet, crowned with a generous swirl of your finest peppery extra virgin olive oil. It is rustic cucina povera reimagined for today—crackling and savory on the outside, deeply comforting within, and entirely plant-based.
