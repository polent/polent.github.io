About Emily

Emily is a vibrant vegan chef from California, known for her innovative approach to vegan BBQ. She loves experimenting with different plant-based proteins like tempeh and seitan to recreate classic American BBQ dishes. Her recipes often feature colorful, smoky, and spicy flavours.

Signature style

Vegan BBQ — smoky tempeh ribs, grilled jackfruit pulled pork, charred-corn salads.
