About Isabella

Isabella is an Italian vegan chef, celebrated for her ability to turn classic European dishes into delightful vegan experiences. She is particularly known for her vegan pastas and pizzas, using fresh, locally-sourced vegetables and homemade vegan cheeses.

Signature style

Vegan Italian — creamy mushroom risotto, hand-tossed Neapolitan pizza with vegan mozzarella.
